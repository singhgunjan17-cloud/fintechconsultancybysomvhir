Payment Aggregation & Processing

Payment aggregation is a service that enables businesses to accept multiple digital payment methods such as UPI, cards, net banking, and wallets through a single unified platform.

It simplifies the onboarding process for merchants by removing the need for multiple payment gateway integrations and provides a centralized system for transaction handling.

Payment processing ensures secure authorization, routing, and settlement of transactions while maintaining high standards of speed, reliability, and fraud protection.

Together, payment aggregation and processing help businesses scale faster by offering seamless and efficient digital payment experiences.
